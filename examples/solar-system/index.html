Planets, truly to scale

The orrery above stylises distance so every orbit fits on screen. Diameter, though, is real here: each disc is sized against Jupiter, the widest planet. Mercury really is that small.

And the Sun? Roughly ten Jupiters across — about 109 Earths edge to edge.
