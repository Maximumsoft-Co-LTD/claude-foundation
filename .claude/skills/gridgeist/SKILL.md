# Gridgeist

Use grid, type, spacing, rules, and product content as one visual language.
Preserve intentional brand, behavior, and accessibility.

Read [visual workflow](references/visual-workflow.md) for composition,
responsive implementation, and rendered review. Compare genuinely different
sketches only when the direction is unresolved; use disposable investigation
prototypes within harness scope.

Judge observable hierarchy, readability, interaction, and responsive states.
Do not treat an image or source-only review as proof of working behavior.
Record consequential choices in OpenSpec and let configured project providers
capture rendered evidence. Follow returned workspace paths and resume routes.
