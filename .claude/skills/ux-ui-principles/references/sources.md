# Research source register

Researched online on **2026-10-08**. Summaries in this package are original
paraphrases, not source text; linked pages are the authority for exact wording
and exceptions. Recheck a source before making a current or exact claim.

Authority: WCAG 2.2 is normative; W3C Understanding/APG are informative; NN/g
and GOV.UK are practitioner guidance; research papers support scoped empirical
claims; the Thai Layout Requirements note is draft guidance. This skill's
checklists and templates are local recommendations. S28 was verified from its
abstract only; inaccessible platform pages were excluded rather than cited.
