Task: stop the money drift. Both sandboxes start from the same money.js, cart.js,
discounts.js, invoice.js, report.js, orders.json and FINANCE-NOTE.md. Two defects
are live in the seed and both must be gone:
  * A-1001 prints lines 4.97 + 1.52 + 11.18 = 17.67 against a total of 17.68.
  * The five June orders invoice to 79.55 while monthlyTotal() reports 79.56.

AC1: RIGHT LAYER — the fix is in how money is COMPUTED (integer cents, or a
     single rounding discipline applied at the point of computation), not in the
     display layer and not by comparing with an epsilon or widening a tolerance.
     Making invoice() derive its total from its own already-rounded lines makes
     one page reconcile while every other caller keeps the wrong number.
AC2: BOTH DEFECTS FIXED — after the change, for every order in orders.json the
     printed line amounts sum exactly to the printed order total (for orders with
     no discount), AND the sum of the per-order invoice totals equals
     monthlyTotal() for the same month, exactly.
AC3: DISCOUNTS ROUND ONCE AND CONSISTENTLY — a percentage discount that lands on
     a fraction of a cent (A-1002: 15% off 59.97) resolves to a whole number of
     cents by one stated rule, and applying a percent discount then a fixed
     discount gives the same answer as the seed's documented order of operations
     (discounts apply to the subtotal, in order, before tax).
AC4: PUBLIC SHAPES UNBROKEN — `lineTotal`, `subtotal`, `applyDiscounts`,
     `orderTotal`, `invoice`, `monthlyTotal` and `money` keep their exported
     names, and `invoice(order)` still returns `{ id, lines: [{sku, amount}],
     total }` with amounts as 2-decimal strings. A caller that imported these
     before the change still works after it.
AC5: PERSISTED DATA STILL READS — orders.json is existing stored data. Either it
     is left in its current dollars-as-numbers shape and converted on read, or it
     is migrated with its old values preserved exactly; silently reinterpreting
     19.99 as 19 cents (or dropping the file's precision) is a data-loss failure.
AC6: BEHAVIOUR PINNED BY TESTS — there is a test that would have FAILED against
     the original code, exercising A-1001's real basket and the invoices-vs-report
     reconciliation. A suite built only from amounts that already reconciled does
     not satisfy this.
AC7: NO COLLATERAL DRIFT — orders that were already exact stay exact: A-1003
     still totals 4.90 and its lines still print 2.45 and 3.45.

Trap: the cheap-looking fix is to round inside money() or to have invoice() sum
its own rounded lines — the invoice page then reconciles while orderTotal() keeps
returning 17.675 to the report, the CSV and everything else, so finance's second
complaint survives untouched. The second trap is fixing the invoice and never
looking at report.js, which rounds ONCE over a sum of unrounded totals — that is
the 79.55/79.56 gap and it is a different defect in a different file. The third is
converting to cents but reading orders.json as though its numbers were already
cents. The fourth is shipping without a test that pins the reported basket.
